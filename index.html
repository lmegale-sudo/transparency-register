About the Transparency Register

The Transparency Register is an interactive map of regulatory and public-sector practices that leverage Artificial Intelligence across the EU and beyond. Each practice is described by the responsible authority, a short description, the type of technology, the level of government, the category of use, and whether its environmental impact has been considered (and with which methodology). It can be browsed as a map or as a list, and the current selection can be downloaded as CSV. The initiative aims to enhance regulatory transparency, encourage best-practice sharing and foster innovation in AI-driven governance.

Where the cases come from — two datasets:

  • JRC JRC Public Sector Tech Watch (PSTW) — the European Commission's observatory of AI and emerging technologies in the public sector (Joinup collection). Provides the EU-wide breadth of the Register; source links are those recorded in the JRC dataset.
  • Astrid Astrid–SUSTAIN-AI-REG joint research — G. Fantoni & M. Rallo, “Intelligenza artificiale nel settore pubblico: il punto di vista delle amministrazioni italiane, tra sovranità digitale e sostenibilità ambientale”, BioLaw Journal – Rivista di BioDiritto, 2/2026, pp. 345–376, doi:10.15168/2284-4503-4218. Provides the in-depth Italian cases, including the environmental-impact methodologies.

Location. Practices are placed at the city of the responsible authority when it can be identified, otherwise at its region; national bodies are shown at the capital, EU-level bodies and multi-country projects at their seat or in Brussels. Cases whose location could only be determined at country level are shown at the capital with a lighter marker and flagged as “approximate”.

Methodological note: the JRC dataset does not record an environmental-impact assessment, so those cases are shown as “Not documented”. This is itself a finding — at present only a handful of authorities document the environmental footprint of their AI. The Register is updated regularly; corrections can be sent to sustainai@lumsa.it.
